## 0. Goal

The CG pipeline uses, per HW-limit branch B of irrep ν and in the joint regime (z = 1+εφ, w₁ = 1+εω₁, w₂ = 1+εω₂, y = w₂y₀), the numbers
  R_Q(B) = det₆[Q_B, Q_vac,(w₁,w₂)]/Ψ_Q^B × det₂^{(1)}[Q_B; y]/minor₁(B),  Qnum = R_Q(B)/R_Q(top) × (known),
and their top-kill partner det₆[Q_B, Q_vac,(w₁,y)]/Ψ_Q^B, to **leading and next-to-leading order in ε**. Question: what are these two orders in terms of *untwisted* objects (the untwisted Q-polynomials of the HW ket of ν with staggered degrees, the untwisted transfer eigenvalues, and the directions φ, ω)? This stage establishes the structure numerically and tests the two natural candidate forms; it does not attempt a proof.

Precision pr = 200, ε ∈ {10⁻⁶, 2·10⁻⁶, 4·10⁻⁶, 8·10⁻⁶} (four-ε Richardson for subleading coefficients), φ = (1/3, −2/7, 5/11) and φ′ = (−2/5, 3/8, 7/11), ω₁ ∈ {3/4, −5/3}, ω₂ ∈ {−1/5, 9/7, −12/5}, P1 (h = 1/2, θ = (2/7, −3/5)). The 6 HW-limit branches only.
