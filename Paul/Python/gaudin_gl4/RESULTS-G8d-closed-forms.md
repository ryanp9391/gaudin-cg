## 1. Conventions
GT pattern ((m₁₁), (m₁₂, m₂₂), (m₁₃, m₂₃, m₃₃), λ), κ = row 3, y_i = κ_i − i, Y_s = λ_s − s, y_ik = m_ik − i in G4's 14-point; d = |λ| − |κ| = ν₄ (depth in the sense of the brief: d = n for λ₄ = 0); ν = μ − λ′; M[row][col]; ξ = x(x+2), x = m₁₂ − m₂₂; V⁽³⁾ = `reduced_op('V', λ′₁₂₃)` on GLNRep(κ). R_i as above (the "+" version: raise κ_i then lower back; the "−" version Π(y_i − Y_s − 1)/Π(y_i−y_j)(y_i−y_j−1) has the same sum). A neighbour with a vanishing denominator is dropped (R_i = 0). The order of W is G3's (κ, row 2) lexicographic.

## 2. D1 — a(κ; λ) and a₄ₐ(κ; λ)

* **Data.** For each of the 150 κ (κ₁ > κ₂ > κ₃) of the six λ the exact (a, a₄ₐ) from the two-point path (κ₂,κ₂),(κ₂+1,κ₂−1) of G3's operators against V⁽³⁾ (`a_data`; lower and upper off-diagonal ratios equal; λ′ = λ, a generic rational λ′ for λ = [6,6,3,0] where V⁽³⁾ vanishes). Printed per λ in the tables file. κ with κ_i = κ_{i+1} have 1×1 paths only (a not separable from b); there the formula's convention R_i := 0 is tested through the full diagonal (§3).
* **(i)** a − 1 = c ΣR_i: c = −1 (1 parameter, 21 κ), 129/129 held-out; the more general "R_i × affine(y_i)" ansatz of the brief gave the same: coefficients (−1, 0). **(ii)** a₄ₐ − d = c₀ + c₁ΣR_iy_i + c₂|κ|ΣR_i + c₃ΣR_i = (0, 2, −1, 4): a₄ₐ = d + Σ_i R_i(2y_i − |κ| + 4). Values: κ = (5,3,1) of [6,4,2,0]: a = 32/35, a₄ₐ = 128/35; (5,2,1): 464/315, 2792/315 (G8b's table, reproduced).
* **Corners.** At κ = λ₁₂₃ (R_i = 0, numerator factor s = i): a = 1, a₄ₐ = d = λ₄ (0 for λ₄ = 0, the "depth 0" fact of G8b); at κ = (λ₂, λ₃, λ₄) (every R⁻_i vanishes) a = 1 too (symmetry κ ↦ c − κ_reversed of G8b).
* **Not a product of linear forms:** a is a sum of three rational functions with poles at κ_i − κ_j = 0, ±1 (the neighbour norms).

## 3. D2 — b₃, b₄ₐ (and the V4b parameters)

### 3.1 What was fitted
Samples: J_ii − a V_ii on 1×1 and 2-point paths over (ν, λ′) for every κ (κ₁ > κ₂ > κ₃) of 42 distinct λ (1052 κ values, 45 exact random (weight space, λ′) samples each, 28 unknown coefficients per κ, rank 28, consistent). Global ansatz per monomial m of (ν₁, ν₂, λ′₁..λ′₄) (total degree ≤ 2): c_m(κ, λ) = P_m + Σ_i R_i S_m, P_m a polynomial in (κ₁, κ₂, κ₃, p₁, p₂, p₃) of weighted degree ≤ 3 − |m| (V₃) / 4 − |m| (V₄ₐ), S_m in (y_i, e₁(y), e₂(y), p₁, p₂, p₃) of the same weighted degree, p_k = ΣY_s^k (symmetric in Y: tested — the unsymmetrised ansatz is also consistent but needs 211 instead of 70 unknowns). Exact rref and greedy sparsification on the κ of 37 λ (the sparsification uses a random subset of 213 / 153 + 60 rows and the result is then checked on every training row), then the 118 κ of the 5 whole λ withheld from the fit (all correct) and finally the 2004 fresh samples of 10 new λ. The tables (`closed_forms_gl4_tables.txt`, "D2") are the closed forms; `b_of(name, κ, λ, ν, λ′)` evaluates them.
### 3.2 Structure
* b₃: ν-quadratic part q₃[ν₁² + ν₁ν₂ + ν₂² + λ′₁ν₁ + λ′₂ν₂ − λ′₃(ν₁+ν₂)], **q₃ = −(d + ΣR_i(y_i + 3))**; the coefficients of λ′₁ and λ′₂ are equal; λ′₄: e₂(κ) + κ₂ + 2κ₃; λ′₄² absent. b₄ₐ: the same bilinear shape (ν₁², ν₂², ν₁ν₂, ν₁λ′₁, ν₂λ′₂ with one coefficient q₄ₐ, ν₁λ′₃, ν₂λ′₃ with −q₄ₐ; verified for all 162 κ of 4 λ, V₃ and V₄ₐ), λ′₄: κ₃(κ₁ + 2)(κ₂ + 1). These coefficients are not Casimir scalars of κ and λ alone: they contain the individual κ_i (through ν_i), and the neighbour sums ΣR_i y_i^p, which are rational in κ with the poles of the neighbour norms.
* **b₄ₐ for κ₃ = 0 is the constant 80 of G8b** at λ = (6,4,2,0) (λ′ = λ); in general it depends on (ν, λ′).
* **Depth 0, λ₄ > 0 (G5/G8b b₄ₐ − b₃ = 18 on [5,3,2,1]²):** at κ = λ₁₂₃ every R_i vanishes and the tables give b₄ₐ − b₃ = λ′₄ · [λ₃(λ₁+2)(λ₂+1) − e₂(λ₁₂₃) − λ₂ − 2λ₃] (all 27 other coefficients cancel) = 18 λ′₄ at λ = (5,3,2,1) (= 18 for λ′ = λ), 28 λ′₄ at (6,4,2,0) (= 0 for λ′₄ = λ₄ = 0), 64 λ′₄ at (7,4,3,2).
### 3.3 Degenerate κ
κ with κ_i = κ_{i+1} have only 1×1 paths; there J_ii = a α_ii + b holds exactly with a, b from the same formulas and R_i := 0 for the invalid neighbour (432/432 tests on [9,6,3,0], [8,5,3,1], [7,4,1,0], [10,7,4,1], [6,6,3,0], [7,7,3,3]).
### 3.4 V4b (what is established)
J^{(4b)} = a′V⁽³⁾ + e[ξ, V⁽³⁾] + b′ + cξ + gξ², g = −e/4: **e = (1 − a)/8** exactly (378 samples over 9 λ, no dependence on ν, λ′), a′ affine in (ν₁, ν₂, λ′) (7 coefficients), c quadratic (28), b′ of total degree 4 (210 monomials; the exact degree-4 fit (210 monomials, rank 210, 260 samples) at κ = (8,5,2) of [9,6,3,0] and the failure of degree 3 are printed by stage `v4b`). Their coefficient functions in (κ, λ) were not identified.

## 4. D3 — where it comes from
Word groups of the 39 universal reduced words of V₃ (at λ′): α = words in the gl(3) generators and E₄₄ (21), β = exactly one E_{i4} and one E_{4j} (18, e.g. (4,3)(3,4); (4,1)(1,4); (2,1)(4,2)(1,4)), γ = 0. For V₄ₐ: 30 / 48 / 0, V4b: 36 / 49 / 0. The κ-block of each group on every path block (|P| ≥ 2) of [4,2,1,0]² (19 blocks, 6 κ) and [6,4,2,0]² (128 blocks, 20 κ), exact fits J_group = a_group V⁽³⁾ + b_group:
| operator | α | β | α + β |
|---|---|---|---|
| V₃ | 1 | a − 1 = −ΣR_i | a |
| V₄ₐ | d = ν₄ (E₄₄ insertions) | a₄ₐ − d = ΣR_i(2y_i − |κ| + 4) | a₄ₐ |
| V4b | e_α = 0 | carries all of e = ΣR_i/8 | a′, e |
(128/128, 19/19; a_group equals the stated value on all κ.) **Mechanism:** the α words stay in the κ-block and are gl(3)-covariant: they reproduce V⁽³⁾ (V₃) or d·V⁽³⁾ (V₄ₐ, each E₄₄ gives the weight d = ν₄). The β words E_{4j}···E_{i4} (E_{i4} acting first) leave the block to κ + e_k (E_{i4} = a gl(3) vector operator, so κ → κ + e_k, k = 1,2,3) and come back (E_{4j} is a covector operator, only κ + e_k → κ closes the loop); on the κ-block the gl(3) words between them act inside V_{κ+e_k}, and by the Wigner–Eckart theorem the result is, per neighbour k, (gl(3)-covariant operator) × (reduced matrix element). The data say that this equals R_k × V⁽³⁾ (V₃: with sign −1; V₄ₐ: with the weight dressing 2y_k − |κ| + 4 of the Cartan insertions — the intermediate state has weight ν + e_k − e₄, and E_kk/E₄₄ pick up y-dependent values), where R_k = |⟨κ + e_k‖E_{k4}‖κ⟩|²-type GT norm: the same Π_s(y_k − Y_s)/Π_{j≠k}(y_k − y_j)(y_k − y_j + 1) that appears in the diagonal elements of E_{4k}E_{k4} in the GT formulas of gl(4) ⊃ gl(3). This explains the pole structure of a and why a = 1 at the corners, and the equality of the two orderings (ΣR⁺ = ΣR⁻). It does not prove the formula: the identification of the β κ-block with R_k V⁽³⁾ is read off from exact data, and the proof would need the gl(3)-covariance argument (the κ-block of a word of one E_{i4} and one E_{4j} between gl(3) words lies in the single multiplicity space of V⁽³⁾-type tensor operators) plus the GT matrix elements of E_{k4}; for V4b the same split puts all of e (the [ξ, ·] term) into the β words.

## 5. D4 — the recursion from closed forms
### 5.1 The matrices (`closed_form_mats`)
For a source pattern p and each of the 21 displacement classes: diagonal a·α + b (α = gl(3) diagonal, §5.2, b from §3); Δm₁₂ = ±1 with κ fixed: a·(up, down of §5.2); 12 hopping classes (Δm₁₂ = ±1, a root move of row 3): `structure_gl4.universal_F` (V₃, V₄ₐ); 6 classes with Δm₁₂ = 0: G4's linear factors × cubic Q (records from `closed_form_one` + `reconstruct_q` of V₃ and V₄ₐ, the six V₃ cubics are in `structure_gl4_tables.txt`; V₄ₐ has its own linear factors; 12 records, cached). **No GLNRep, no E_ij matrix is touched.** Exactly equal to `weight_space_mats` on: P_small ∪ P_big (328 weight spaces, 13 018 entries per operator, V₃ and V₄ₐ), 20 random weight spaces of [9,6,3,0]² with generic non-dominant λ′ (denominators 1,2,3,5,7), and consistent with [V₃, V₄ₐ] = 0 on the whole W (new: 156 spaces) also at λ = (30,20,10,0).
### 5.2 The gl(3) chain with general λ′ (`gl3_chain`)
With t = m₁₁, s = m₁₂ + m₂₂, m₁ = m₁₂, m₂ = s − m₁, x = m₁ − m₂, ξ = x(x+2), κ = (k₁,k₂,k₃), q = λ′₁₂₃:
 up = M[m₁+1, m₁] = −(k₁−m₁)(k₂−m₁−1)(k₃−m₁−2)(t+1−m₂)(t−m₁+q₁−q₂)/((x+1)(x+2));
 down = M[m₁−1, m₁] = −(k₂−m₂)(t−m₁)(k₁−m₂+1)(k₃−m₂−1)(t+1−m₂+q₁−q₂)/(x(x+1));
 α = A₀ + g ξ + K′/ξ, g = 3s/16 − (k₁+k₂+k₃)/8 + (q₁+q₂)/8 − q₃/4 + 3/8, K′ = (s−2k₂)(s−2t)(s−2k₁−2)(s−2k₃+2)(2−s+2t+2q₁−2q₂)/16 (K′/ξ := 0 at x = 0), A₀ a cubic polynomial in (s,t,k,q) with 57 terms (tables file). For q = κ these are the λ = λ′ forms of `jacobi_cg` (with μ₁ = t + q₁). Exact vs `GLNRep(κ)` and `reduced_op('V', q)` on **614 weight spaces (51 with ≥ 3 path points) with generic rational λ′, 1571 entries, 0 mismatches** (the shapes of u, d, α were fitted by exact linear algebra on 80 + 260 samples and are independent of the checks).

## 6. Surprises and corrections
1. **a is not mysterious**: a = 1 − ΣR_i, the sum of the GT norms of the neighbours (G8b: "not a product of linear forms; by Wigner–Eckart a sum over the neighbours — not derived"). G8b's table values are reproduced.
2. **b₃, b₄ₐ are degree-2 polynomials in (ν, λ′)** (G8b: "quadratic in ν, affine in λ′" at one κ — also the cross terms ν·λ′ are bilinear, there is no λ′²: confirmed in all 28 coefficient functions); their coefficients are not products of linear forms but P + ΣR_iS.
3. **The gl(3) chain for general λ′ is as simple as at λ = λ′**: only the last factors change, (t − m₁ + q₁ − q₂), the twist factor of G4's hopping formula, and α depends on q through g, K′ and the 57-term cubic A₀.
4. **Pole dropping**: the formulas hold for κ with equal neighbouring entries only with the invalid neighbours removed from the sums; this is empirical (432 tests).
5. e = (1 − a)/8 and g = −e/4: the V4b commutator coefficient is the V₃ β-coefficient up to a constant; a′ is not constant along the sectors (G8b: "a, e functions of κ"): a′ depends on (ν, λ′) affinely; only e is a function of (κ, λ).
6. V₃ and V₄ₐ commute on the whole weight space W, not only on M_μ (156/156 spaces of the 12 products, plus λ = (30,20,10,0)).
7. The 2-operator recursion is fully determined on 222/222 weight spaces but with larger bottom levels (n₀ = 1 on 101 instead of 188); two copies of [6,4,2,0]² need V4b.
8. G3's `copies` (not G8a's recursion) is the faster baseline: the comparison "recursion ×8 faster than the eigen-decomposition" of G8a refers to the slice computation after the eigenvalues; the eigenvalues cost the same in both.

## 7. Not done / limits
* **V4b**: the coefficient functions of a′ (affine), c (quadratic), b′ (quartic) in (ν, λ′) and the quartic Q's of the five remaining Δm₁₂ = 0 classes (G4 has one; each reconstruction takes about 3 min) were not identified/reconstructed; so `closed_form_mats` has no V4b, and the closed-form recursion uses (V₃, V₄ₐ) — complete for 295/297 and 1592/1592 copies tested, incomplete where two copies share (v₃, v₄ₐ).
* **Eigenvalues** still from G3's PRV matrices (m×m, built by applying the words to PRV vectors in GLNRep(λ)); no matrix-free way to select the physical eigenvalues of W₁ was found. The whole-product timing is therefore not matrix-free.
* The tables of b₃, b₄ₐ (183 and 487 terms) are fits (exact linear algebra, held-out verified), not derived; the symmetric-in-Y structure and the degree bounds are empirical; I did not search for a shorter form beyond the greedy sparsification. A derivation of b from the word groups (α, β) would explain the tables (§4).
* D3: the β κ-block is read off, not proved; the formula of the β reduced matrix element as R_k × V⁽³⁾ (with the V₄ₐ dressing) is verified on 147 path blocks only for |P| ≥ 2.
* G4's Δm₁₂ = 0 records are cached from the symbolic term sums (representation-free) but their evaluation of removable poles uses G4's Laurent-along-a-line device; 0 pole skips and 0 mismatches were observed.
* Only (V₃, V₄ₐ, V4b-structure): no gl(5)/general-N statement, no proof.

## 8. Usage
`python -X utf8 closed_forms_gl4.py all` (about 6 min; also `a`, `b`, `words`, `gl3chain`, `v4b`, `mats`, `det`, `slices`, `slices9`, or `run a,mats`); tests `python -X utf8 test_closed_forms_gl4.py` (3 s once `closed_forms_gl4_qcache.pkl` exists, else + 3 min). API: `a_of(kappa, lam)`, `a4a_of(kappa, lam)`, `b_of(name, kappa, lam, nu, lamp)` (name 'V3' or 'V4a'), `gl3_chain(kappa, lamp3, nu123)`, `gl3_entries`, `closed_form_mats(lam, lamp, mu)` → (patterns, {'V3': M, 'V4a': M}), `slices_closed(lam, lamp, mu)` → [(triple, slice {pattern: mpf}, residual)], `words_product`, `word_group`, `weight_patterns(lam, nu)`, `v4b_sample`. The labels alone suffice, e.g. `closed_form_mats((30,20,10,0), (30,20,10,0), mu)` for any dominant mu = ν + λ′ with a non-empty weight space (`scale_demo()`).
