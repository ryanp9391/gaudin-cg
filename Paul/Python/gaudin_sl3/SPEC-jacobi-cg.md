## 0. The question

Round 6 delivered (R) the slice engine — for every irrep μ of V_Λ⊗V_Λ the m-dimensional space M_μ of PRV/Zhelobenko slices inside the w-dimensional weight space V_Λ[μ−Λ], the exact m×m matrix V_red of the Gaudin vertex operator on it
(its eigenvectors are the SoV copies, eigenvalue V = α₁ − η₁/2), the full HW vectors by a level recursion; (Q0, S) the Q-system spectrum at scale (the V's, 3 s for all of [14,7,0]², no n_W dependence) and S's "echelon Baxter pencil",
itself a tridiagonal three-term recursion, linear in b/2; (S) the ε-series/jet-formula route to the slice (20–85 s per copy); (D) the Gauss dictionary. **R observed that V_red, as the formula-defined operator on the WHOLE weight space V_Λ[μ−Λ]
in the GT basis, is tridiagonal in every sector** (421/421 at [28,14,0]), and Jacobi (strictly positive off-diagonal products) in the w = m sectors.

**The question of this work package.** For a tridiagonal (Jacobi) matrix the eigenvector of eigenvalue V is the sequence p_k(V) generated by the three-term recurrence. If the CG slice of each multiplicity copy is such a sequence evaluated at its
Q-system eigenvalue V, then "Clebsch–Gordan coefficients from Q-functions" reduces to: solve the Q-system for V (done at scale) and run a recurrence — n_W-free, as fast as the slice engine, bypassing the ε-series and the two-vacua kernel entirely.
**Establish whether this is true, in what form, and with what closed forms.**

## 1. Conventions (binding; copied from R, S, Q0)

* One site: GT states in the order of `gl3_reps.GL3Rep(Λ).patterns` = ascending lexicographic in (m₁,m₂,t) of the pattern {{t},{m₁,m₂},{λ₁,λ₂,λ₃}} (t the singleton row; the HW state is the LAST index); `GL3Rep`/`slice_engine.Rep` matrices act on
  kets as columns; plain rational non-unitary GT normalisation (`gl3_reps._raise/_lower`: E₁₂ t→t+1 with coefficient −(t−m₁)(t−m₂+1); E₂₁ t→t−1 with coefficient 1; E₂₃, E₃₂ move m₁ or m₂ by ±1 with the rational coefficients of `_raise(2,·)`, `_lower(2,·)`;
  targets outside the interlacing range are dropped); E₁₃ = [E₁₂,E₂₃], E₃₁ = [E₃₂,E₂₁]; weights (E₁₁,E₂₂,E₃₃) = (t, m₁+m₂−t, |Λ|−m₁−m₂).
* Slice of a HW vector Ψ of weight μ: ψ_top = Ψ[·,q_top] ∈ V_Λ[μ−Λ] (site 1; site 2 = the HW state). **M_μ = {v ∈ V_Λ[μ−Λ]: E₁₂^{λ₁−λ₂+1}v = 0, E₂₃^{λ₂−λ₃+1}v = 0}** (Zhelobenko/PRV), dim M_μ = m = Racah–Speiser multiplicity.
* **V_red** (R): V_red = ½ Σ_{σ,τ∈S₃} sgnσ sgnτ E_{σ₁τ₁}E_{σ₂τ₂}Φ_{σ₃τ₃}, Φ_cc = λ_c, Φ_ab = −E_ab (a<b), Φ_ab = 0 (a>b), product ordered as written (E_{σ₁τ₁} acts last). It is a weight-zero operator on the weight
  space W := V_Λ[μ−Λ] (dimension w) and leaves M_μ invariant; on M_μ it is the exact m×m matrix of R, eigenvalue V = the Q-system's pencil root (Q0's V, S's b through V = q − b/2 − η/2, q = (μ₁+2)(μ₂+1)μ₃/2, η = η₁ = |Λ|²+cas₂(Λ)−cas₂(μ)/2).
* Copies: eigenvectors of V_red on M_μ, ordered by increasing V; the slice is scaled so that **its first nonzero component (ascending GT index) is exactly 1**. Indices in R's files are 1-based in the GL3Rep order, in the slice_engine API 0-based global GT indices.
* Q0's files: Laurent data per copy, QPOLY = monic echelon q₁,q₂,q₃ (ascending powers, 30 digits; deg q_k = M_k = 2λ₁−μ_k+(k−1)); a₁ = V+η/2, b₁ = 2(α−a₁), α = (μ₁+2)(μ₂+1)μ₃/2. S's pencil: `pencil_poly(lam,mu)` = exact polynomial in b whose roots are the branches (gcd over ALL three echelon systems).
* Precision: exact arithmetic (`Fraction`/sympy) for the chain coefficients and all closed-form checks; mpmath 30–50 digits for eigenvector comparisons; float64 only for the speed claims. Report failures with numbers, not adjectives.
